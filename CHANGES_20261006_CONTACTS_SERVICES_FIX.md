# VyaparOS – Contacts + Services Fix

## Changes
- Import contacts button is now embedded inside the Party Name field, styled like the Add Item unit button.
- The Import contacts button disappears after contacts are successfully imported.
- Imported contacts remain searchable in the Party Name dropdown.
- Add Item > Services can now be saved without Purchase Price or stock values.
- Services are saved with unit `Service`, purchase price `0`, stock `0`, and category `Services` when no category is entered.
- Product validation remains unchanged: product items still require both purchase and selling price.
